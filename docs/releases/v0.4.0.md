Place the webcam and the screen exactly where you want them.

- **Webcam over the screen:** choose **Overlay** in the editor. The screen fills the whole video with no
  margin, and the webcam sits on top with rounded corners. Drag it anywhere in the preview and drag a corner
  to resize it.
- **Custom layout:** choose **Custom** to move and resize both the screen and the webcam in the preview. It
  starts from the side-by-side layout, and **Reset** takes you back there.
- **Snapping:** dragged boxes snap to the edges, the margin and the middle of the video. Hold Alt to place
  them freely.
- **Align the webcam side by side:** line the webcam up with the **Top**, **Centre** or **Bottom** of the
  screen.
