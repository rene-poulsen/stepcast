Record just yourself, and make videos for Reels, TikTok, Shorts and more.

- **Webcam only:** choose **Desktop**, **Desktop + webcam** or **Webcam** on the start screen. A webcam-only
  recording captures nothing from your screen, and the camera fills the whole video.
- **Formats for every platform:** pick Original, 16:9 (YouTube), 9:16 (Reels, TikTok, Shorts, Snapchat),
  4:5 or 1:1 (feed posts), 3:4 or 4:3. Each choice on the start screen remembers its own format.
- **Frame yourself:** a guide on the webcam preview shows what stays in the video, and the editor's
  **Framing** slider moves the crop left or right.
- **Change it afterwards:** in the editor, switch a desktop + webcam recording to show only the desktop or
  only the webcam, and change the format. Vertical and square videos put the webcam above or below the screen.
- **Tidier webcam layout:** the screen now has the same margin to the edge as the webcam has, so the video
  looks balanced on both sides.
